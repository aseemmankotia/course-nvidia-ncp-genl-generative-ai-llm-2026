# Chapter 11 Lab: Evaluating LLMs and Mitigating Hallucination, Bias, and Toxicity

**Estimated time:** 25 minutes

## Exercise

Configure NVIDIA NeMo Guardrails Colang rails (topical rail + fact-checking rail) around a sample chatbot flow using a provided config, and test with an off-topic and a factually-risky prompt

## Key takeaway

Choosing the right evaluation method and guardrail type—matched to the specific quality or safety risk—is what the exam tests in this domain.
